# 0.7.106: Blank rows take the wait

**A finished line now leaves as soon as it ends, and the blank row after it takes the pause.** With vertical Scroll and Wait for line on, a run that has blank rows between two text lines no longer holds the finished line in place. It leaves the moment that line ends, glides through the blanks and stops on the last one, which waits in the current slot with the next line in the slot below it. One ordinary scroll then brings that line in 0.1 s before its first word.

**The reported cue.** Four visible lines, current line 2, 0.18 s at 0.7x (one ordinary scroll takes 0.257143 s), Pause line length 0.5. "All right, that's $160" ends at 15.226667 s, followed by two authored blank rows, then "and we'll get this" at 16.875 s. 0.7.105 held "…$160" in place until 16.175 s. Now it leaves at 15.226667 s, and the two-row run settles the second blank in the current slot at 15.655238 s. The blank waits there until 16.517857 s, when an ordinary scroll settles "and we'll get this" at 16.775 s. Its first word highlights at 16.875 s.

**Same rate.** Every run still eases in, cruises at 1.5 rows per Transition duration ÷ Speed and eases out, and each extra row adds two thirds of an ordinary scroll. The approach after the wait is exactly an ordinary scroll.

**Short gaps.** When the gap is too short to stop on the last blank and then make an ordinary scroll, the run carries straight on through the blanks without stopping, overlapping or speeding up. It settles at most Transition duration ÷ Speed ÷ 3 before its usual arrival, so up to 0.1 s plus that before the line's first word. In the same saved document, "putting it off." ends at 8.548333 s with one blank before "Got a quote from." at 8.975 s. Waiting would need 0.514 s, so the two-row run carries straight on and settles at 8.977 s, just after the first word, whose highlighting keeps its own time.

**Unchanged.** Lines with no blank between them still hold until the scroll is due, or leave when the line before ends, as in 0.7.105. The opening run through the leading slots has no line to leave, so it still waits and settles the first line 0.1 s before it is spoken. Trailing blanks still go as soon as the last line ends. Fade, Slide and Pop, Wait for line off and horizontal pages are unchanged, and seeking lands on the same frame as playing.

The compiled adapter smoke test now compares Pause Line Length at 3.2 s, while Auto (two blank rows) and 2 s (one) are still running different row counts away from "Third.".
