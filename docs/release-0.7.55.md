# S-Captions 0.7.55

Word timing can now cross a line ending. When a native caption clip is grouped into lines, the selected line's word lane also shows the closest word of the previous and next line, outlined and dimmed. A line's first and last words can be dragged or stepped into the gap before the neighbouring line, and the neighbouring word's own edges can be trimmed to make room. Edges still stop at a touching neighbour instead of pushing it.
